The rule behind this stepAn objective is answerable and tied to the decision — "find out how much students will pay, and which of two benefits moves intention most", not "understand students". Every section and every question must trace back to one objective, or it is cut.

3Length, language and demographics

About 4 minutes to answer.

Demographics at the end (only the ones you will cross by):

The scale IS the decision (5.4)NOMINAL (a list, for frequencies), ORDINAL (a rank, for the median), INTERVAL (a balanced labelled rating, for the mean and the t-test) or RATIO (a number with a true zero). Pick the highest the concept honestly supports. Ratings are balanced and labelled at every point, always in the same direction.

How to put it in Google Forms Prompt 2 gives you the questionnaire in two forms. Use whichever you prefer:
  1. Fast — run the script: open script.google.com → New project → delete the sample code → paste the Apps Script the assistant gave you → press Run → authorise with your Google account the first time. It creates the Form in your Drive and prints its link in the Execution log (View ▸ Logs).
  2. By hand: open forms.new and add each question from Part 1, using the question type the assistant labelled (Multiple choice, Checkboxes, Linear scale, Short answer…), its options, and Required where marked.
  3. Open the Form, press Preview (the eye), answer it once on a phone, and time it. Fix anything that fails the checks below before you send it.
